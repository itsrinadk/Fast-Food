Sides:

French Fries: Crispy, golden-brown potato fries seasoned with salt, served hot and typically enjoyed alongside burgers. They come in various cuts and styles, such as shoestring fries, steak fries, or curly fries.

Onion Rings:

Sliced onions coated in batter or breadcrumbs and deep-fried until they become crispy and golden. They offer a savory taste and are often served with dipping sauces like ketchup or aioli.

Side Salad: A fresh, leafy salad consisting of mixed greens, tomatoes, cucumbers, carrots, and other vegetables. It provides a lighter option for those seeking a healthier side to pair with their meal.

Sweet Potato Fries: Similar to traditional French fries but made from sweet potatoes. These fries offer a slightly sweeter taste and are often seasoned with various spices or served with dipping sauces like honey mustard or aioli.

Cole Slaw: A salad made from shredded cabbage and carrots, mixed with a tangy dressing, which could be mayonnaise-based or vinegar-based. It serves as a refreshing side dish, balancing the richness of burgers with its crisp and tangy flavors.

Mozzarella Sticks: Breaded and deep-fried sticks of mozzarella cheese, served hot and gooey inside with a crunchy exterior. They are typically served with marinara sauce for dipping.

Potato Chips: Thinly sliced and fried potato chips, often seasoned with salt or various flavors. They offer a crunchy, portable side option.
